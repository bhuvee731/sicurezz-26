About the Symposium

SICUREZZ'26 is an Annual Tech Extravaganza organized by the Department of Cyber Security at K.L.N. College of Engineering (KLNCE). The symposium brings together aspiring innovators, young developers, problem-solvers, and creative thinkers from diverse domains of Engineering. It offers a premier dynamic platform to showcase technical skills, compete, and gain insights from security professionals.
